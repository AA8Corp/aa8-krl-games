# Kids Robotics Lab — Standing Facilitator Brief (July 2026)

This brief applies to **every** July session. Week-specific content lives in the three lesson plans.

**Weekly Focus:** Python Programming Fundamentals with Physical Robotics (building toward future smart robot projects)
**Session Length:** 6:00–8:00 PM
**Staffing:** Lead Instructor + 2 Assistants
**Target Group:** Beginner students (approximately ages 9–15)

---

## Lesson Objectives (month-level)

By the end of each session, students should be able to:

- Explain what variables and simple decision-making are in programming.
- Write and modify basic Python code in Replit.
- Understand how code eventually controls physical robots.
- Successfully complete a guided coding challenge.
- Practice teamwork and debugging skills.

## Safety Reminders

- No food or drinks near laptops.
- Walk — no running in the classroom.
- Respect equipment and classmates.
- Keep cords taped down or out of walkways.
- If robotics hardware is used:
  - Power off equipment before making adjustments.
  - Keep fingers clear of moving parts.
- If drones are demonstrated:

## Quick Brain Break (3–5 Minutes): Robot Freeze Challenge

1. Students spread out safely.
2. Instructor calls out robot commands:
   - "Forward"
   - "Turn Left"
   - "Turn Right"
   - "Scan"
   - "Charge Battery"
3. Students perform robotic movements.
4. When the instructor says **"System Freeze!"**, everyone must stop instantly.
5. Resume after a countdown from three.

This provides a fun movement break while reinforcing robotics vocabulary. **July tie-in:** these are the exact commands students will type in Week 3's turtle robot — call that out during the game.
